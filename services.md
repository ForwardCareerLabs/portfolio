# Security, Risk & Resilience Services

This page summarizes the types of work represented by the portfolio examples.

## Security Questionnaire Support

Review security questionnaires, identify unsupported or ambiguous claims, map responses to available evidence, and flag gaps that require validation before submission.

## Risk Assessment & Risk Register Support

Translate operational or technical concerns into structured risk statements with likelihood, impact, treatment direction, ownership, and follow-up actions.

## Incident Response & Tabletop Exercises

Build scenario-based exercises that test decision-making, communications, escalation, continuity, and recovery instead of merely walking participants through a checklist.

## Operational Resilience & Business Continuity

Identify dependencies, failure points, recovery priorities, and practical actions that improve an organization's ability to continue critical operations during disruption.

## Security Documentation

Turn vague or inconsistent security information into clear, reviewable documentation for internal teams, customers, vendors, and compliance stakeholders.

## Working approach

The focus is on evidence, clarity, and defensible claims. Unknown information is identified as a gap rather than filled with assumptions.

Career-document work remains a separate capability, but this repository is intentionally centered on cybersecurity, GRC, risk, and operational resilience.
