Security Answer Improvement Example

Example 1

Before  
We use encryption to protect data and secure our systems  

After  
Sensitive data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry standard methods. Access is restricted based on role and monitored for unauthorized activity  

Why this works  
Moves from vague statement to a clear and defensible answer that holds up during review  

---

Example 2  

Before  
We have processes in place for handling security issues  

After  
Security incidents are identified, contained, and investigated using a defined response process. Actions are documented and reviewed to improve future response  

Why this works  
Turns a generic claim into a structured process that sounds real and complete
